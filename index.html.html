Efficiency Tips

Expert advice on products, timing & smart techniques.

The Top-to-Bottom Rule

Always dust high surfaces first before vacuuming or wiping floors so falling debris gets picked up later.

Dwell Time Matters

Spray cleaners and let them sit for 3–5 minutes before wiping. Let the product do the heavy lifting for you!

Color-Coded Cloths

Use blue for glass, yellow for bathroom, green for kitchen. Prevents cross-contamination and boosts speed.

Microfiber Magic

Damp microfiber picks up 99% of bacteria with just water. Reduce reliance on harsh chemical cleaners.
